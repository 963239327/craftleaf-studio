Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a State Medical Board photo and an ERAS Residency photo?

State Medical Boards and ECFMG require strict 2x2 inch (1:1 square, 600x600 px) biometric passport photos with a white background and neutral expression, whereas MyERAS requires a 2.5x3.5 inch (5:7 ratio, max 375x525 px, <=150 KB JPEG) professional headshot where smiling in business attire is encouraged.

Can I wear my white medical coat against a white background?

If wearing a white lab coat for ERAS, choose a soft light-grey backdrop swatch so your shoulders don't vanish into pure white; for State Board 2x2 passport photos, wear a dark navy/charcoal blazer or shirt instead.
