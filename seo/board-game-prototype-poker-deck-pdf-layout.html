Why US Letter Height (279.4 mm) Trips Up 3×3 Poker Grids

  • 3 rows × 88.9 mm = 266.7 mm tall, leaving only 6.35 mm (0.25 in) top and bottom margin on 11-inch US Letter paper! If you add 3 mm gaps between rows, Row 3 gets clipped by printer hardware margins. That's why CardSheet defaults gapMm = 0 for shared single-cut interior lines that fit US Letter and A4 cleanly!
  • Designing full graphic art before Playtest #1: Use rapid text-mode cards for your first 5 balance playtests so changing numbers takes 10 seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 9 standard 2.5x3.5 inch poker cards fit on one 8.5x11 US Letter sheet without shrinking?

Yes! Three columns equal 7.50 inches (leaving 0.50 in side margins on 8.5" paper) and three zero-gap rows equal 10.50 inches (leaving 0.25 in top/bottom printer margins on 11" paper). Just be sure to select 'Actual Size / 100%' when printing.

Can CardSheet also generate a printable tuckbox to hold my finished prototype deck?

Yes! Click the '3D Standee & Tuckbox Studio' tab inside CardSheet (../cardsheet/index.html?preset=tuckbox) and enter your card count + sleeve/GSM thickness to print a custom die-line tuckbox.
