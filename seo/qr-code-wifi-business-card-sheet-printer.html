How to Make Home-Printed Business Cards Feel Like $60 Print-Shop Stock

  • Skip Perforated Tear-Out Sheets: Micro-perforated store sheets leave fuzzy 'postage-stamp' nibs along every edge. Instead, print on solid 80 lb – 110 lb Smooth Cover Cardstock (216–298 gsm) and slice cleanly with a slide/guillotine paper trimmer!
  • Keep 5–6 mm Safe Margin: Our business-us preset enforces a clean 6 mm inner text safe zone so contact details never crowd the trim edge.

2 Common DIY Business Card Mistakes

  • Printing heavy solid-black ink backgrounds on home inkjet printers: Heavy dark saturation warps paper fibers and smudges — stick to crisp dark-navy/slate typography on heavy bright-white or natural cream cotton stock for an executive letterpress aesthetic!
  • Scaling 'Fit to Page' in Adobe/Chrome: Shrinks 3.5×2.0" down to 3.32×1.89"; always print at 100% scale.
