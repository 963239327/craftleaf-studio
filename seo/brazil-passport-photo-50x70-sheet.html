Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Brazilian passport photo 5x7 cm or 2x2 inches?

Brazilian Consulates specify 5x7 cm (50x70 mm) on the RER passport form (though several US-based Brazilian consulates also accept 2x2 inch photos — 50x70 mm fits the official RER box natively).

How do 4 photos of 50x70mm fit on one 4x6 inch (10x15cm) print?
