Frequently Asked Questions

What size photo is required for Indian Passport renewal in the UK, Canada, or Australia?

Even in countries that normally use 35x45mm (UK/Australia) or 50x70mm (Canada), VFS Global Indian Embassies and High Commissions strictly require 2x2 inch (51x51 mm) square photos!

Does OCI require a white background or light non-white background?

Traditional OCI guidelines ask for a plain light non-white background (such as soft grey or pale blue) to distinguish from white form paper, though some VFS missions accept white. Always check your local VFS checklist — our tool offers Pure White, Soft Grey, and Light Blue swatches.

Does the 600x600 px export meet indianvisaonline.gov.in e-Visa specs?

Yes! India's e-Visa portal requires a square 1:1 image between 350x350 px and 1000x1000 px, making 600x600 px right in the sweet spot.
