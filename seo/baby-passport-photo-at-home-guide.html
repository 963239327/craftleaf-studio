The 3-Minute White Bedsheet Trick for Perfect Baby Passport Photos

  1. Iron or pull taut a plain white bedsheet or swaddle blanket on a firm mattress near a bright window (no direct sunbeams across the face).
  2. Dress baby in a solid dark or colorful onesie (not white!) so shoulders stand out clearly, and remove hair bows, hats, and pacifiers.
  3. Stand directly above looking straight down (90° perpendicular, avoid standing over the window so your own body doesn't cast a shadow on baby!).
  4. Load your favorite calm shot into our Baby Passport Studio above, center the head inside the relaxed infant guide oval, and slide White-Background Boost (55–75%) to smooth minor cotton sheet creases into clean studio white!

Top 3 Baby Passport Photo Rejections

  • A parent's hand propping up the baby's neck at the edge of the frame: Instant rejection. Always use the flat lay-down bedsheet method or hide supporting hands completely underneath the white sheet.
  • Car seat buckle straps showing over shoulders: Drape the plain white sheet over the entire car seat BEFORE placing baby on top.
  • Uploading baby photos to sketchy free cloud websites: Keep your child's biometric portrait 100% private in offline browser memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do a newborn baby's eyes have to be wide open for a US passport photo?

The US State Department specifies it is acceptable if an infant's (particularly a newborn's) eyes are not open or entirely open, provided the baby faces forward against a plain white background with no other person or object visible.
