How to Prepare Korean Visa, K-ETA & Residence Card Photos

  1. Wear a dark collared top or crew neck (Korean immigration explicitly flags white shirts blending into white backdrops) and remove colored contact lenses, glasses, and hats.
  2. Brush bangs slightly aside so both eyebrows and ears are clearly visible.
  3. Open the 35×45mm Studio above and position your chin and crown inside the 32–36 mm green bands.
  4. Download the free 413×531 px digital file for K-ETA or Korea Visa Portal e-Visa, or print an 8-up 4×6" sheet for consular visa applications and your in-Korea ARC biometric appointment.

Strict Korean Immigration Photo Rules Most Applicants Miss

  • Colored or circle contact lenses: South Korea's Ministry of Foreign Affairs explicitly bans tinted/circle lenses that alter natural iris size or color because automated e-gates match iris/periocular biometrics.
  • Heavy K-beauty studio retouching: Over-smoothed jawlines or enlarged eyes from portrait apps cause airport Smart Entry / ARC rejections when live face scans fail to match.
  • Using an older passport's exact photo for ARC registration: Korean immigration requires a photo taken within 6 months that does NOT duplicate an old passport page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are colored contact lenses or glasses allowed in a South Korea visa photo?

No. Korean MOFA strictly prohibits colored/circle contact lenses, sunglasses, and eyeglass frames that obscure eye contours or cause flash reflections.

What size photo do I need for the Korean Alien Registration Card (ARC / Residence Card)?

Korea Immigration Service requires the exact same 35 mm x 45 mm white-background biometric photo (head length 32–36 mm) taken within the last 6 months.

How do I resize the free PNG under 100 KB for the K-ETA website?

Our 413x531 px output already stays under K-ETA's 700x700 px ceiling; simply export or save as standard 80%-quality JPEG in Preview/Paint to bring file size well under 60 KB.
