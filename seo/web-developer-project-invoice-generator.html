Billing a Fixed-Price Quote in Stages? Check Per-Line Allowance Before Sending the Next Milestone Invoice

When billing from separate invoice files, an unpaid sent invoice already consumes its deliverable allowance while an unsent draft does not — and a project still at $850 of $1,000 can hide a $50 overage on one deliverable line. Test your next draft locally in browser RAM against one accepted quote (stable Line IDs, 1 currency, pre-tax amounts): missing mappings, credit notes, or changed quotes hold for manual review rather than being guessed, and no invoice is edited or sent.

Protect Your Engineering Cashflow

  • Waiting until 100% project completion to send Invoice #1: Always invoice 40–50% upfront before writing production code.
  • Bundling cloud hosting pass-through costs with labor without itemizing: List AWS/Vercel/Apple Developer pass-through expenses on their own row.
