Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Japan visa photo 45x45mm or 35x45mm?

Japanese Visa application photos are now 45 mm x 45 mm square (updated by MOFA), whereas Japanese domestic passports for Japanese citizens remain 35x45 mm.

Can I use a 2x2 inch US passport photo for a Japan visa?

Some consulates tolerate trimming down a 2x2 inch (51x51mm) photo ONLY if the head still measures ~32mm after trimming, which often chops off your shoulders. Using our native 45x45mm preset guarantees exact proportions.
