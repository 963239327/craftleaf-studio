Best Practices for Faster Enterprise Consulting Payouts

  1. Reference the client's Purchase Order (PO #) or Master Services Agreement (MSA) date directly in the Document # or Client Details block.
  2. Group hourly work into clear outcome buckets (e.g., 'Discovery & Stakeholder Interviews — 6.5 hrs') rather than vague 'Consulting hours'.
  3. Use the 'Deposit / Paid Deducted' field to show prepaid retainer credits cleanly while highlighting the exact remaining Balance Due.
  4. Include complete ACH/Wire routing or SWIFT coordinates in the Payment Instructions box so corporate treasury can wire funds without emailing back.

3 Mistakes That Delay Corporate AP Approval by 30+ Days

  • Addressing the invoice only to your project contact instead of 'Accounts Payable / Finance Dept' alongside the project sponsor's name.
  • Rounding fractional hours inconsistently — our engine computes exact decimal quantities (e.g. `14.25 × $185`) to the cent.
  • Missing tax/VAT treatment note for international advisory engagements (e.g. 'Reverse Charge Services' for EU B2B clients).
