Pro Scoring & Folding Tools That Prevent Cracked Ink Creases

  • Score BEFORE Folding (The Dead-Ballpoint Trick!): Heavy cardstock cracks along fold lines if bent cold. Lay a metal ruler along dashed valley-fold guides and press firmly with a bone folder, butter-knife back, or an empty/dry ballpoint pen before creasing — folds snap 90° like factory retail boxes!
  • Grain Direction Tip: Fold parallel to paper grain when possible for smooth spines; use PVA white craft glue or 6mm double-sided tape (Scor-Tape) on tuckbox side glue flaps rather than wet glue sticks that pop open in dry winter air.
  • Exact Sleeve Depth Math: Bare 300gsm cards ≈ 0.32 mm/card · Penny-sleeved ≈ 0.45 mm/card · Premium 100-micron matte sleeves ≈ 0.62–0.68 mm/card (remember trapped air adds ~15% until compressed!).

2 Classic DIY Tuckbox Sizing Disasters

  • Zero tolerance math (54 cards × 0.30 mm = 16.2 mm exact): Zero-clearance boxes bulge and tear thumb flaps! Always add +2.0 to +3.0 mm breathing clearance so the deck slides in smoothly.
  • Printing tuckboxes for large 70+ card sleeved decks on 1 sheet when the net exceeds 210mm width: Split oversized commander decks into two 2-part telescope trays or dual chapter boxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do A-frame paper standees require plastic clip bases?

No! Our A-frame die-line includes integrated bottom base flaps that fold underneath into a stable self-standing triangle (you can either interlock the slit tab dry for temporary D&D encounters or dab a drop of glue for permanent minis).

How do I calculate tuckbox depth in millimeters for a sleeved 54-card deck?

Measure your stacked deck lightly compressed with a ruler in millimeters and add 2 mm of finger clearance (typically ~18–20 mm unsleeved, ~26 mm in thin sleeves, or ~36 mm in thick 100-micron sleeves).

Why is the top portrait upside-down on the printed standee template?

Because the strip folds 180 degrees over the top head-crest crease! Printing the back panel inverted ensures both sides stand right-side-up once folded over the apex.
