Frequently Asked Questions

      
Why does ChatGPT only print 1 page when I press Ctrl+P in Chrome or Edge?
Web chat apps wrap messages inside nested height: 100vh; overflow-y: auto flex containers, which causes browser print engines to clip everything scrolled off-screen. CleanPrint flattens scroll containers into clean paginatable document flow.
Does CleanPrint read or upload my private AI conversations?
Never. CleanPrint uses minimal activeTab Manifest V3 permissions — DOM cleanup runs 100% locally inside your active tab only when you click the button, with zero external servers.
Can I delete one embarrassing typo prompt from the middle of a chat before exporting PDF?
Yes! CleanPrint's interactive preview lets you remove individual blocks or turns before saving your final PDF.
