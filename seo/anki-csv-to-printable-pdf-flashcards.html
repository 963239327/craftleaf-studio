Supported Paste Formats & Duplex Alignment Physics

  • Anki Plain-Text / TSV Export: In Anki Desktop → File → Export → Notes in Plain Text (.txt) (uncheck HTML/tags if desired), then paste straight into CardSheet!
  • Quizlet Copy-Paste: On any Quizlet set → ⋯ More → Export → Copy text (uses Tab between Term & Definition) — parsed automatically!
  • Pipe Quick-Syntax: Front Prompt | Back Answer | Deck Tag | Optional Icon — zero spreadsheet needed.
  • Why Back Columns Must Mirror (Col 1 ↔ Col N): When a sheet flips along its long edge, the top-left card on Page 1 lands at the top-right of Page 2! CardSheet automatically reverses column order on every even page AND lets you dial ±0.5 mm X/Y printer tray drift.

3 Reasons DIY Printable Flashcards Fail (And How We Fix Them)

  • Show-through ink bleed on thin 75–80 gsm copy paper: Answers ghost through thin paper under desk lamps! Always load 110 lb Index (199 gsm) or 65 lb Cover cardstock so fronts stay opaque.
  • Drawing boxes on BOTH front and back sides: Even 0.8 mm mechanical roller play looks crooked if both sides have tight borders — CardSheet keeps clean centered back margins so backs always look factory-centered!
  • Browser 'Fit to Printable Area' shrinking cards by 4%: Always expand More Settings in Chrome/Edge/Safari print dialog and lock Scale = 100%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I export an Anki deck so it pastes cleanly into CardSheet Studio?

In Anki Desktop, click the gear icon next to your deck -> Export -> choose 'Notes in Plain Text (.txt)' without HTML media tags, open the .txt file, copy the tab-separated lines, and paste directly into CardSheet Studio.

Should I select 'Flip on Long Edge' or 'Flip on Short Edge' in my printer dialog?

For portrait sheets, select 'Flip on Long Edge' (standard book-style duplex); CardSheet mirrors horizontal column order accordingly so every card's front aligns directly behind its matching back.

How many cards can I batch-layout at once?

You can preview and print multi-card sheets right away for free, and CardSheet Pro ($9 lifetime key) unlocks unlimited multi-page CSV deck batching plus custom printer X/Y offset presets.
