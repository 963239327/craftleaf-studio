Tutor Cancellation Policy & Parent Scholarship / ESA Receipt Tips

  • 24-Hour Attendance Clause: 'Please provide 24 hours notice to reschedule; missed sessions with <24h notice are billed at 50%–100% of reserved slot.'
  • Prepaid Monthly Tuition: Bill at the 1st of the month for that month's reserved weekly slots ('Due by the 5th of the month') rather than chasing arrears!
  • State ESA / Homeschool Reimbursement Ready: Parents using Arizona/Florida/Texas/Utah Education Savings Accounts (ClassWallet / Step Up) require itemized PDF Paid Receipts showing student name, subject taught, lesson dates, tutor credentials, and $0.00 balance!

  • Billing after the month ends: Switching to prepaid 4-session or 10-session packages eliminates unpaid ghosting and cut-short exam seasons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this receipt format work for ClassWallet / Step Up For Students ESA reimbursements?

Yes! State ESA programs typically require an itemized PDF receipt showing instructor name, parent/student name, specific academic subject, dates of instruction, rate, and 'PAID RECEIPT' confirmation — all built right in.

How do I bill 45-minute or 90-minute lessons?

Either bill per session (e.g., Qty 4 sessions @ $65.00) or use decimal hours (0.75 for 45 minutes, 1.5 for 90 minutes).
