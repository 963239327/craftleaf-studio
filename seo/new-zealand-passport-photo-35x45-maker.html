How to Pass New Zealand's Online & Paper Photo Checks

  1. Stand 0.5–1m away from a plain light-colored wall (so your body casts zero wall shadow) while someone photographs you from 1.5m away.
  2. Take off glasses, keep eyes level and mouth closed, and ensure hair doesn't fall across your eyes.
  3. Open the NZ 35×45mm Studio above — our 'Soft Grey (#F7F8FA)' swatch avoids the 'Background too white / low contrast' error on passports.govt.nz!

Why passports.govt.nz Rejects Pure-White Studio Cutouts

  • Pure #FFFFFF background error: Uniquely, New Zealand DIA's online checker flags over-bleached #FFFFFF backgrounds because pale ears/hair lose edge contrast! Keep Background Boost gentle (25–40%) with the Soft Grey swatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did New Zealand's online passport checker reject my pure white background photo?

New Zealand DIA explicitly advises against pure stark white backgrounds because automated edge detection needs subtle contrast around light skin or grey/blonde hair. A plain light grey or off-white wall passes much more reliably.

What size is a printed New Zealand passport or visa photo?

Printed New Zealand passport and visa photos are standard 35 mm wide by 45 mm high, fitting 8 photos onto a single 6x4 inch print sheet.

Are glasses allowed in NZ passport or NZeTA photos?

No. Glasses must be removed for both New Zealand passport applications and Immigration NZ visa/NZeTA photos.
