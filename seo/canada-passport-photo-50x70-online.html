How to Prepare Compliant Canadian 50×70mm Photos

  1. Take a well-lit, front-facing portrait with squared shoulders and a neutral closed-mouth expression (no teeth showing).
  2. Launch the Canada 50×70mm preset above and align your crown and chin inside the 31–36 mm biometric guide bands.
  3. Check that plenty of upper shoulder and white headroom remain visible — Canadian photos deliberately leave more margin around the head than European 35×45mm prints.
  4. Download the single 591×827 px digital PNG for online PR portals for free, or unlock the 4-up 4×6" sheet to cut four crisp 50×70mm prints.

Important IRCC Commercial Photographer Stamp Note

  • Paper passport vs. online PR portal: While digital Express Entry / PR portals accept clean DIY digital uploads, physical mail-in Canadian passport applications require studio name/date/guarantor endorsements on the back of one print — verify current IRCC instructions for your application channel.
  • Flash glare on forehead or cheeks: Diffuse window light outperforms direct phone flash every time.
  • Tilted shoulders: Keep both shoulders level and facing directly toward the camera lens.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does your 4x6 sheet fit 4 Canadian 50x70mm photos while other tools only fit 2?

Placed upright (827px tall), only one row of two 50x70mm photos fits on a 1200px-tall 4x6 sheet. Our layout engine rotates each 50x70mm tile 90 degrees (827x591 px), packing a 2x2 grid of 4 full-resolution 300 DPI photos!

What is the exact pixel size for a digital Canada PR photo at 300 DPI?

At 300 DPI, 50x70 mm equals 591 x 827 pixels, meeting IRCC's minimum 420x540 px digital upload requirement with extra sharpness.

Can I smile in a Canadian passport or PR photo?

No. IRCC strictly requires a neutral facial expression with eyes open and mouth closed — no smiling or frowning.
