Frequently Asked Questions

How much do UGC creators charge for Paid Ad Usage Rights on an invoice?

Industry standard adds +30% to +50% of the base video fee per 30 days of Paid Media / Spark Ads usage (or +100%–150% for a 6-to-12 month buyout), listed as its own licensing line item.

Do brands require an LLC to pay a UGC creator invoice?

No — brands pay both Sole Proprietors (using your legal name + free IRS EIN on Form W-9) and LLCs, as long as your PDF invoice matches the legal name on your W-9.

Can I send a Pitch Rate Card / Campaign Quote first?

Yes! Select 'Quotation / Estimate' in the Document Type dropdown to send an itemized campaign package quote during negotiation, then flip it to 'Invoice' once signed.
