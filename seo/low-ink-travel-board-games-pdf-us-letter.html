Never Use 'Shrink to Fit' When Mixing US Letter & A4 Files

  • Printing an A4 PDF on US Letter at 'Fit to Page' shrinks cards to 94% (59.7 × 83.5 mm), making them rattle loosely inside standard 63.5×88.9 mm card sleeves! Always print our dedicated US-Letter file at 100% in North America, and our A4 file at 100% elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do European A4 Print-and-Play PDFs get cut off on US printers?

ISO A4 is 17.6 mm taller (297 mm vs. 279.4 mm) and 5.9 mm narrower than North American 8.5x11 US Letter paper. Printing A4 at 100% on US Letter chops 8.8 mm off the top and bottom, which is why we master separate native US Letter and native A4 editions.

Can I print the Low-Ink edition on a black-and-white monochrome laser printer?

Yes! We designed distinct iconography (suits, solar terms, heat pips, critter emblems) alongside color cues so even monochrome laser prints remain crisp and playable.
