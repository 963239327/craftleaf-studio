Prepare Your Entire AOS Packet's Photos in 3 Minutes

  1. Snap a fresh, glasses-free portrait in front of a well-lit white wall within 30 days of mailing your USCIS Lockbox packet.
  2. Open the USCIS 2×2" Studio above and align head height (1.0" to 1.375" / 25–35 mm) and eye level (1.125" to 1.375" from bottom).
  3. Export the 1800×1200 px 4×6" sheet and order TWO standard 4×6" glossy prints ($0.78 total at CVS/Walgreens) to yield 12 crisp 2×2" photos.
  4. Trim cleanly along the outer corner ticks, lightly write your Name and A-Number on the back with a soft #2 pencil or felt pen (do not dent the front!), and place each pair in small labeled zip-top bags paperclipped to each form.

Lockbox Mail-In Photo Handling Rules

  • Stapling directly through the face of the photo: Never staple through a USCIS photo! Place 2 photos in a small clear plastic pouch and staple/clip the pouch to a blank sheet behind the form.
  • Ballpoint pen indents bleeding onto the glossy emulsion: Use a soft felt-tip marker or gentle pencil and let dry completely before stacking.
  • Sending only 2 photos for a 3-form concurrent filing: USCIS routes I-485, I-765, and I-131 to separate adjudication queues; each form needs its own pair of 2×2" prints.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many 2x2 photos do I need when filing I-485, I-765, and I-131 together?

Include 2 photos for I-485, 2 for I-765, and 2 for I-131 (6 total for the applicant), plus 2 photos of the petitioner and 2 of the beneficiary if concurrently filing spousal Form I-130 (8–10 photos total).

Should USCIS photos be printed on glossy or matte 4x6 photo paper?

USCIS accepts thin photo-quality glossy or matte paper, though standard glossy drugstore photo paper (Fuji Crystal Archive / Kodak at CVS or Walgreens) produces the sharpest biometric contrast.

What should I write on the back of USCIS 2x2 photos?

Lightly print your full name and Alien Registration Number (A-Number, if you have one) on the back of each photo using a pencil or felt pen so you do not crease the portrait.
