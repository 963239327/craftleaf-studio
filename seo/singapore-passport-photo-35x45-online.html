Common Singapore MyICA Automated Rejections

  • Cropping out upper shoulders: ICA rejects tight face-only crops where shoulders aren't fully visible on both left and right edges.
  • Spectacles or reflective skin shine: Wipe oily forehead/nose glare with a tissue before shooting and take off all eyeglasses.
  • Mirrored iPhone front-camera selfies: Never submit mirrored portraits (asymmetrical moles/hair parts flipped); use rear camera or click our '⇄ Mirror' button if your front camera flipped the shot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does MyICA accept 413x531 px when the example says 400x514 px?

Yes! Both 400x514 and 413x531 share the exact 35:45 (0.778) aspect ratio, and MyICA's online cropper accepts sharp images above 400x514 px up to 2 MB.

Can I wear glasses for a Singapore ICA passport photo?

No. ICA advises removing spectacles completely to prevent frame obstruction and lens reflection rejections at automated immigration lanes.

What is a 4R photo sheet in Singapore?

In Singapore, Hong Kong, and Malaysia, '4R' is the standard 4 x 6 inch (102 x 152 mm) postcard photo size — our 1800x1200 px 4x6 sheet prints 8 exact 35x45mm ID photos on one S$0.50 4R print!
