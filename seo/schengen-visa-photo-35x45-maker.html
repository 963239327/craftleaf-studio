Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Schengen visa photo the same size as a US passport photo?

No. Schengen visa photos are rectangular 35x45 mm (1.38x1.77 inches) with a 70–80% face fill, whereas US passport photos are square 51x51 mm (2x2 inches) with a smaller 50–69% head ratio.

How many 35x45mm photos fit on a 4x6 inch photo print?

Eight 35x45mm photos fit comfortably in a 4x2 grid on a 6x4 inch (1800x1200 px @ 300 DPI) sheet with generous margins and corner scissor cut ticks.

Does this 35x45mm size also work for Australia, Singapore, and New Zealand visas?

Yes! 35x45 mm is the international ICAO 9303 standard shared across the EU Schengen Zone, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and Korea.
