3 Classic German Foto-Mustertafel Rejections

  • Head too small (< 32 mm): German mission clerks hold a transparent plastic Mustertafel stencil directly over your print — if your chin and crown don't reach the 32–36 mm tick marks, it's rejected on the spot.
  • Slight head tilt or over-the-shoulder angle: Both ears/cheeks must be symmetrical ('Frontalaufnahme').
  • Print scaling on dm/CEWE Sofortdrucker: Turn off 'Randlos-Zoom' if offered or verify the printed 25.4mm / 1-inch ruler bar before cutting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I still print my own 35x45mm photos for a German Visa, Blue Card, or Chancenkarte?

Yes! Germany's domestic Bürgeramt E-Passfoto rule applies to in-country ID offices; German Embassies, Consulates, TLScontact/VFS centers worldwide, and the digital.diplo.de visa portal continue to use standard 35x45mm Foto-Mustertafel portraits.

How much does printing an 8-up 10x15cm sheet cost at dm or Rossmann in Germany?

A single 10x15 cm (4x6 inch) CEWE/Kodak instant photo print at dm-drogerie markt or Rossmann costs around €0.27 to €0.35 and yields 8 exact 35x45mm Passbilder.

Are glasses permitted under the German Foto-Mustertafel?

Even though clear prescription glasses without reflection are technically allowed only if eyes and eyebrows are 100% unobstructed, German missions strongly recommend removing glasses to avoid frame-shadow rejections.
