Why US Mexican Consulates Reject Pharmacy Photos

  • Bringing 2×2 inch US photos: Mexican visa stickers and consular forms require narrow metric sizes (35×45 mm Tamaño Pasaporte or 26×32 mm Tamaño Infantil — trim an extra 4.5mm margin if your specific consulate requests Infantil!).
  • Wearing small stud earrings or bangs over forehead: Mexican consular officers strictly enforce 'sin aretes' (no earrings) and 'frente descubierta' (clear forehead).
  • Smiling with teeth: Keep lips gently closed in a neutral expression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I still need printed photos for a Mexican Passport renewal at a Consulate?

Most modernized SRE offices and Mexican Consulates now take digital passport photos on-site during your biometric appointment, whereas Visa applications (Temporary/Permanent Residency, Tourist Visa) and certain minor/notarial forms still require printed photos.

What is 'Tamaño Pasaporte' in millimeters and inches?

In Mexico, 'Tamaño Pasaporte' measures 3.5 cm wide by 4.5 cm tall (35 x 45 mm, or 1.38 x 1.77 inches) — rectangular, not square like US 2x2 inch photos.

Are earrings allowed in Mexican consulate visa photos?

No — Mexican SRE photo standards specify uncovered ears without earrings, piercings, glasses, or headwear.
