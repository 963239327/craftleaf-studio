Hardscape Deposit Rules, Underground Utility Clauses & Tax Tips

  • Site Clause: 'Estimate valid 30 days. Public utilities marked via 811; homeowner responsible for marking private irrigation lines/dog fences.'
  • Plant Warranty Note: 'Nursery stock guaranteed true to species upon planting; ongoing watering care transfers to property owner.'

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I measure items in Cubic Yards, Visits, Square Feet, or Lots?

Yes! Write the unit clearly in the description (e.g., 'Hardwood Mulch Installed — Cu. Yds') and enter the numerical quantity (6 or 4.5) in Qty.

How do I bill HOA or commercial property managers Net-30?

Set Payment Terms to 'Net 30 Days', add the HOA property name & PO/Contract # in Client Details, and include your business ACH or check mailing address.
