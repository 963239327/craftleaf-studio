Essential Live Entertainment Booking & Overtime Clauses

  • Date-Hold Retainer Rule: '50% non-refundable retainer locks event date on calendar; remaining Balance Due 14 days prior to event date.'
  • Day-Of Overtime Rate: 'Same-night overtime approved by client/planner on-site billed at $175 per half-hour.'
  • Power & Weather Rider Note: 'Requires dedicated 15A/20A grounded circuit within 25 ft and covered shade/rain protection for outdoor setups.'
  • Musician & DJ Tax Deductions: Speakers, mixers, controllers, instrument strings/maintenance, record pool subscriptions, stage attire, liability insurance (COI), and venue mileage.

Lock Event Dates & Collect Final Balances Before Showtime

  1. Send a 'Quotation' PDF with base performance + optional Ceremony Audio / Uplighting / Photo Booth add-ons when couples or event planners inquire.
  2. Switch to 'Invoice' to collect your 50% date-hold retainer.
  3. Two weeks before the wedding or corporate event, enter the paid retainer in 'Deposit / Paid Deducted' to send the final Balance Due PDF (never chase envelopes on the wedding dancefloor!).
  4. Issue a 'Paid Receipt' PDF for corporate event planners and university/municipal finance offices.

Never Collect Final Wedding Balances After Midnight Load-Out

  • Making Balance Due 'Night of Event': Exhausted couples and coordinators forget checkbooks at midnight — industry standard is Balance Due 7–14 days BEFORE the event date.
  • Giving away separate ceremony PA systems for free without listing value: Even if bundled, list 'Ceremony Wireless Mic & PA Setup' + a package discount so clients appreciate the extra rig!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I show the 50% booking deposit already paid on the final pre-event invoice?

Keep all performance and lighting packages listed at full price so the couple/planner sees the full contracted scope, then enter the deposit amount in 'Deposit / Paid Deducted' to show the remaining half due.

Do corporate event planners and universities require a W-9 and PDF invoice?

Yes — corporate and campus procurement departments require a formal numbered PDF invoice displaying your legal name/LLC, address, EIN, event date, and ACH/check remittance instructions.
