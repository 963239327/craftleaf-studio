Frequently Asked Questions

What size is a Chinese visa photo in inches and millimeters?

A standard Chinese visa and passport photo is 33 mm wide by 48 mm tall (approximately 1.30 x 1.89 inches), which is narrower and taller than a 2x2 inch US photo.

Can I wear glasses or earrings in a Chinese visa photo?

No. Glasses, tinted lenses, hats, large earrings, and lip/brow piercings should be removed, and both eyebrows and ears must be completely visible.

How many 33x48mm Chinese visa photos fit on a 4x6 print?

Eight 33x48mm photos fit neatly in a 4x2 grid on one 4x6 inch (1800x1200 px) drugstore print with scissor trim lines.
