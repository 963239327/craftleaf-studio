6 Mandatory Customs Clearance Fields to Include on Export PDFs

  • 1. Shipper / Exporter & Consignee / Importer full legal names, street addresses, phone numbers, and EORI / EIN / Tax ID!
  • 2. 6-to-10 digit Harmonized System (HS Code) and Country of Origin right inside each item's description row.
  • 3. Explicit Incoterms 2020 (e.g., EXW, FOB Shenzhen, DAP London, or DDP) + Reason for Export (Commercial Sale or Commercial Sample — Not for Resale).
  • 4. Never write $0.00 value on samples! Customs rejects '$0.00' items — state fair nominal customs value (e.g. $15.00) and note 'No Charge Sample — Value for Customs Purposes Only' in Notes.
  • 5. Proforma Invoice Mode: Select Quotation (or prefix Doc # with PI-2026-001) when buyers need a Proforma Invoice to open a T/T wire transfer or import permit before shipment!

Top 3 Triggers for DHL / FedEx Customs Holds

  • Vague commodity descriptions ('Electronics' or 'Merchandise'): Always specify *what it is*, *what it is made of*, and *what it is used for* (e.g., 'Anodized Aluminum Laptop Stand — HS 7616.99 — Origin: Taiwan').
  • Missing Consignee phone/email or EORI number on EU/UK shipments: Carrier brokers cannot contact the importer to clear duties without phone + EORI/VAT.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Proforma Invoice and a Commercial Invoice?

A Proforma Invoice (PI) is a preliminary commitment/quote sent BEFORE shipment so the buyer can arrange T/T wire payment or import licensing, whereas a Commercial Invoice (CI) is the final binding customs valuation document accompanying the shipped goods.

Can I upload this exported PDF to DHL Paperless Trade and FedEx Electronic Trade Documents (ETD)?

Yes! Browser vector PDF output produces a tiny (<60 KB), crisp, machine-readable A4 or US Letter PDF accepted directly by DHL, FedEx, and UPS customs portals.

How should I invoice free engineering prototypes or PR samples for customs?

List the realistic fair market/production unit cost on the line item so customs can assess classification honestly, and add 'Sample Provided Free of Charge — Value Declared for Customs Purposes Only' in the Notes block.
