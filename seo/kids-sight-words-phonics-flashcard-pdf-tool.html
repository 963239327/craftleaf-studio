Kid-Proof Laminating & Corner-Rounding Tips for Classroom Centers

  • 3mil Thermal Laminator Trick: Print on 160–200 gsm cardstock, cut cards apart FIRST, arrange in a 3mil thermal laminating pouch with 4 mm clear spacing between cards, run through a $25 home laminator, and trim sealed waterproof edges — lasts for years of sticky fingers & dry-erase markers!
  • 4mm Corner Rounder Punch: Clip sharp 90° cardstock corners with a $6 4mm corner-rounder punch so toddler/kindergarten hands never get poked.
  • Emoji Visual Anchors: Include any standard emoji (🐸, 🚀, 🍎, 🦖) in your text or icon column for instant cheerful visual cues without hunting clip-art licenses!
