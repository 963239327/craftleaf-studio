High-Retention GRE / SAT Vocabulary Card Formula

  • Include Part of Speech + Connotation (+/-) on Back: Many GRE Text Completion traps hinge on whether words like *fastidious*, *enervate*, or *sanguine* carry positive or negative tone!
  • Sample Line to Paste: Enervate | (v. [-]) To weaken or drain of mental/physical vigor (NOT energize!) — 'The humid afternoon enervated the marathoners.' | GRE Verbal
  • Use the 3-Pile Leitner Desk Method: Green Pile = instant <2s recall (review Friday) · Yellow Pile = hesitated (review tomorrow) · Red Pile = missed (keep in pocket today!).

How to Print 100 Crisp 3×5" Index Cards Without Printer Jams

Why did Microsoft Word print the wrong back on my 2-column flashcards?
