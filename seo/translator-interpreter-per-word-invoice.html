LSP Agency Payment Terms, Rush Minimums & Interpreter Cancellation Rules

  • LSP Agency vs. Direct Client Terms: Direct clients 'Net 14 Days' · Language Service Providers (LSPs) 'Net 30 Days — Reference PO #'.
  • Minimum Project Fee: Establish a $65–$95 minimum fee for micro-jobs under 400 words so administrative overhead never loses money.
  • Interpreting Cancellation Clause: '2-hour half-day / 4-hour full-day minimum; cancellations <24–48 hrs prior to assignment billed at 100%.'
  • Translator Tax Write-Offs: SDL Trados / MemoQ licenses, ATA membership & certification exam fees, glossaries, headset/booth gear, and Wise currency conversion fees.

Avoid Multi-Currency & Fuzzy-Match Billing Errors

  • Letting agencies round weighted word counts down arbitrarily: Breaking New Words, 75–94% Fuzzy, and 95–99% Repetitions onto explicit lines prevents disputes.
  • Omitting PO numbers on large LSP portals: Language agencies batch-pay strictly by PO # — put the PO # right in Document # or Client Details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the rate field handle small per-word decimals like $0.12 or €0.09?

Yes! Enter any decimal per-word rate (0.12, 0.085, 0.14) multiplied by exact word counts (6420) with accurate two-decimal rounding (and zero-decimal integer rounding when JPY ¥ is selected!).

Can I switch off the Chinese labels when billing a European or US translation agency?

Yes! Simply change the 'PDF Labels' dropdown from 'Bilingual (EN + 中文)' to 'English Only' in one click.

How should certified translators invoice USCIS document translations?

Bill per page or per certificate (e.g. 'Certified Translation of Birth Certificate + Certificate of Accuracy') rather than per word so formatting stamps/seals is compensated fairly.
