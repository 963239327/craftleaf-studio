How to Issue a Clean Contractor Payment Receipt

  1. Open the Contractor Paid Receipt template above — 'Paid Receipt' mode is already selected.
  2. Enter the service performed, your business/1099 details, and the client's company name and address.
  3. Set 'Deposit / Paid Deducted' equal to the total amount paid so the summary displays Total Paid and a verified $0.00 Balance Due.
  4. Record the payment date and wire/check/Zelle confirmation code in Payment Instructions, then click 'Print / Save Vector PDF'.

What makes a contractor receipt valid for business expense reimbursement?

A valid business receipt clearly states 'PAID RECEIPT', a unique receipt/document number, payment date, vendor name & address, buyer name, itemized services rendered, total paid, and $0.00 remaining balance.
