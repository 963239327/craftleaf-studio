How to Issue a Clean Contractor Payment Receipt

  1. Open the Contractor Paid Receipt template above — 'Paid Receipt' mode is already selected.

  3. Set 'Deposit / Paid Deducted' equal to the total amount paid so the summary displays Total Paid and a verified $0.00 Balance Due.
