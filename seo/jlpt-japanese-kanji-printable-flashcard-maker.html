Frequently Asked Questions

Why does A7 (74x105 mm) produce zero paper waste on A4 cardstock?

By ISO 216 geometry, folding or cutting an A4 sheet (210 x 297 mm) in half three times yields exactly eight A7 rectangles (74.25 x 105 mm) — just 1 lengthwise cut and 3 crosswise cuts with zero border waste!

Do Japanese Furigana/Kana, Kanji, and accented Pinyin tones print sharply?

Yes! Browser vector print-to-PDF embeds native OS Unicode outlines (Hiragana, Katakana, Kanji, tone-marked Pinyin like shēngdiào, Hangul, and Cyrillic) at full printer resolution.
