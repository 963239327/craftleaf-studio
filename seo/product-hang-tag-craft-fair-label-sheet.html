Craft-Market Packaging Tricks That Look Boutique on a Shoestring

  • Kraft Brown Cardstock Magic: Printing crisp black ink on 200gsm natural brown Kraft cardstock costs ~$0.01/tag and immediately gives artisan soaps, apothecary jars, and baked goods an organic boutique look!
  • Leave Top Hole-Punch Clearance: Keep an 8 mm top safe margin (pre-configured on our square-50 preset!) so your 3mm hole punch for cotton baker's twine never clips your logo.
  • Double-Sided SKU + Care Info: Put brand/scent/price on the Front and ingredient list, allergen notice, or washing care icons cleanly aligned on the Back!

Create & Trim 60 Boutique Hang Tags in 5 Minutes

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I set custom millimeter dimensions like 40x70mm for tall clothing hang tags?

Yes! Choose 'Custom Millimeter Size (W x H mm)' in the CardSheet preset dropdown, enter 40 width and 70 height, and the grid engine automatically computes the maximum sheet yield and cut lines.

How do I keep the hole punch from cutting into my tag title?

Our Square 50x50mm preset defaults to a generous 8 mm safe margin — simply punch your 3mm twine hole centered 4–5 mm below the top edge.

Can I print different scents or prices on the same sheet?

Yes! Each line of text (or each dropped image) populates its own individual tag cell on the sheet, so one 12-tag page can mix 4 Lavender, 4 Cedarwood, and 4 Citrus tags at once.
