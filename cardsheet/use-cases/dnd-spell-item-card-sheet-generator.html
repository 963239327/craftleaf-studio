Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题解答 (FAQ)

Will these 9-up RPG spell cards fit standard 9-pocket trading card binder pages?

Yes! The default 63.5 × 88.9 mm (2.5 × 3.5 inch) Poker preset matches standard TCG cards, fitting both card sleeves and 9-pocket character spellbook binder sheets.

How does the 3D Paper Standee generator keep the back side upright when folded?

CardSheet Studio automatically rotates the upper back panel 180 degrees across the top mountain-fold crease, so when you fold the strip into an A-frame triangle and glue the base tab, both front and back stand right-side up.

Is my homebrew campaign text uploaded anywhere?

Never. CardSheet Studio runs 100% locally inside your browser tab with zero backend servers.
