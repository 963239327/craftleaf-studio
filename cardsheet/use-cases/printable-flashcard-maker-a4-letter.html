Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题解答 (FAQ)

How do I print double-sided flashcards so the front word matches the right back definition?

Enable 'Generate Mirrored Duplex Back Sheets' with Long-Edge Flip in CardSheet Studio. Column 1 on the front automatically mirrors to Column N on the reverse side so every term aligns with its exact definition when flipped.

Can I import my existing Anki, Quizlet, or Google Sheets vocabulary list?

Yes! Export your deck or spreadsheet as CSV or Tab-Separated Values (TSV) and click 'Import CSV / TSV / JSON' or paste it directly into the Flashcard Designer box.

What paper weight is best for homemade study flashcards?

Heavyweight 180gsm to 220gsm (110lb index) matte white cardstock prevents ink bleed-through during active recall quizzing and feeds smoothly through standard home inkjet and laser printers.
