Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题解答 (FAQ)

Why did my printed Poker cards come out slightly smaller than 63.5 × 88.9 mm?

Browser and PDF print dialogs often default to 'Fit to Printable Area' (around 94–96% scale). Always select 'Actual Size' or 'Custom Scale: 100%' in your system print dialog so physical millimeters remain exact.

Why are outer-margin guillotine ticks better than full grid lines?

Full grid lines leave visible ink streaks along card edges if your blade is off by 0.2mm, whereas corner ticks inside the sheet vanish after your first cut. Outer-margin guillotine ticks keep your slide trimmer aligned from first cut to last without putting ink on your cards.

Can I print multiple copies of common cards without duplicating image files?

Yes! In CSV/JSON or Pipe Designer mode, add a 5th column (e.g. `Strike | Deal 3 DMG | Basic | ⚔️ | 4`) to automatically expand card quantities across paginated sheets.
