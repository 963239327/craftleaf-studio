3-Step Zero-Waste Home Printing Workflow

三步零废纸家用高清打印与直刀裁切流程

  1. Drop your `3.5 × 2 in` PNG/JPG business card design into **Drag & Drop Images** (or customize the built-in Minimal Studio template).
  2. Set copies to `10` to fill an entire 2×5 sheet automatically, and enable **Duplex Back Sheets** if printing a logo or QR code on the reverse.
  3. Print on 250gsm–300gsm matte cardstock at 100% scale and slice along the outer-margin guillotine ticks.

Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题解答 (FAQ)

Why cut plain heavyweight cardstock with guillotine marks instead of perforated Avery sheets?

Micro-perforated sheets leave fuzzy nibs on card edges and jam if printer margins drift by 1mm. Printing onto solid 250gsm–300gsm cardstock and making 7+3 straight slide-trimmer cuts gives true print-shop smooth edges at a fraction of the cost.

Can I switch to European 85 × 55 mm or Japanese 91 × 55 mm business card sizes?

Yes! Select 'Custom Millimeter Size (W × H mm)' in the preset dropdown and enter 85 × 55 mm or 91 × 55 mm — the 2×5 10-up centered grid and guillotine ticks recalculate instantly.

Is single-sheet 10-card front printing free without watermarks?

100% free! You can export high-resolution single-sheet vector PDFs and 300 DPI PNGs with zero watermarks and zero login.
