3-Step Zero-Waste Home Printing Workflow

三步零废纸家用高清打印与直刀裁切流程

  1. Open **3D Standees & Tuckbox** in CardSheet Studio and pick **Folded Paper Standees** or **Parametric Deck Tuckbox**.
  2. For Tuckboxes, enter your card dimensions (e.g. `63.5 × 88.9 mm`), card count (e.g. `54`), and cardstock thickness (`200gsm`, `250gsm`, or `Sleeved`).
  3. Verify your print scale with the built-in `50.0 mm` calibration ruler bar, cut solid black lines, score dashed blue lines, and fold!
  1. 进入工坊顶部的 **3D 折纸立牌与卡盒刀版** 模式，自由切换三角立牌或参数化卡盒。
  2. 制作卡盒时，只需填入卡牌尺寸（如 `63.5 × 88.9 mm`）、收纳张数（如 `54 张`）以及裸牌或套牌厚度，系统自动算出毫厘不差的盒脊厚度与半圆拇指扣槽。
  3. 打印后可用左下角自带的 `50.0 mm` 比例尺核验 1:1 尺寸，沿黑色实线裁切、蓝色虚线压痕折叠粘贴即可成型！

Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题解答 (FAQ)

How is tuckbox depth calculated for sleeved vs. unsleeved cards?

CardSheet Studio multiplies your card count by the selected per-card thickness (0.25mm for 200gsm cardstock, 0.31mm for 300gsm core stock, or 0.46mm for sleeved cards) and adds 1.8mm–2.2mm of finger clearance so decks slide in smoothly without bulging.

Can I export the tuckbox template as an editable vector SVG for Adobe Illustrator, Inkscape, or Cricut/Silhouette?

Yes! With CardSheet Pro unlocked, click 'Download 1:1 SVG Die-Line' to save a clean millimeter-unit vector SVG with separate solid cut paths and dashed score lines—ideal for adding custom box art in Inkscape/Illustrator or cutting on a vinyl/craft plotter.

Do I need plastic standee bases for the paper miniatures?

No plastic bases required! Each standee includes an integrated bottom base flap and hashed glue tab that folds into a rigid, self-standing triangle—or you can snip off the bottom flap to slot into plastic standee clips.
