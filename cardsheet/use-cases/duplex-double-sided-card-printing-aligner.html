3-Step Zero-Waste Home Printing Workflow

Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题解答 (FAQ)

Which way do I adjust ±X mm if the back side printed too far to the left?

Looking directly at the printed Back side right-side up: if the back artwork sits 1.5mm too far left relative to the front cuts, set Back Horizontal Shift to +1.5 mm to push the back layout right.

Should I choose Interleaved or All Fronts → All Backs?

Use 'Interleaved (Front 1 → Back 1 → Front 2…)' when your printer has built-in automatic 2-sided printing. Use 'Grouped (All Fronts → All Backs)' if you manually re-insert the stack into a single-sided printer tray.

Does bleed help even after calibrating duplex offset?

Adding 1.0mm to 1.5mm of bleed alongside duplex calibration absorbs tiny sheet-to-sheet roller variance (~0.2mm) so dark-bordered card backs never show a white sliver.
